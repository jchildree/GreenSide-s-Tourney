Privacy Policy — The Colosseum

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Summary

The Colosseum is a desktop application that helps administrators configure, draft, and publish custom video game tournaments. This policy explains what data the application requests and how it is used.

Data we request

  • Google Forms access: OAuth permission to edit a specific Google Form template so the app can update tournament details. Only the minimum scope necessary is requested.
  • Challonge credentials: Provided by the administrator to push the finalized bracket. Stored locally in an encrypted store or OS keychain.
  • Tournament metadata: name, dates, player lists — entered by the administrator to operate the app.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and third‑party credentials are stored locally on the administrator's machine (OS keychain or encrypted local store). The application does not transmit or store tokens on external servers by default.

Data sharing

The app does not sell or share data with third parties. Data is only sent to the services you authorize (Google Forms and Challonge) to perform the requested actions.

Scope & purpose of Google access

The app requests only the scopes needed to update a specific Google Form template (to set title, date/time, and form questions) and to read responses if required. Tokens are used only on the local machine to perform those actions and are not published publicly.
