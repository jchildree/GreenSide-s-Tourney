Privacy Policy

Application: The Colosseum - Last updated: June 13, 2026

Overview

The Colosseum is a desktop application for managing custom video game tournaments. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The app collects only information you enter directly:

  • Tournament details - name, game, date/time, player limits, stream link
  • Player information - player names and Discord handles submitted via Google Forms
  • Team and draft data - team names and draft picks you configure
  • Account credentials - OAuth tokens for Challonge and Google (see below)
  • App preferences - theme and background image

How Information Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to our servers because we do not operate any servers.

  • Tournament, player, and team data is stored in a local application data file on your computer.
  • OAuth credentials (Challonge and Google tokens) are stored in an encrypted file on your device using Electron's safeStorage API, which uses your operating system's built-in encryption (DPAPI on Windows). These credentials are never transmitted to anyone other than the respective service (Challonge or Google).

How Information Is Used

Information you enter is used solely to:

  • Display your tournament dashboard within the app
  • Update your Google Form with tournament details via the Google Forms API
  • Create and update your Challonge bracket via the Challonge REST API

No information is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app's features.

Third-Party Services

The app connects to these external services on your behalf:

  • Challonge (Fandom, Inc.) - receives tournament name, team names, player names, dates, and stream link to populate your bracket. See Fandom's Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Alphabet Inc.) - receives tournament configuration data to update your Google Form. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not control how these services handle data once it is transmitted to them. Your use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party beyond the two services listed above, and only as needed to operate the features you use.

Analytics and Telemetry

The Colosseum does not include any analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry. No usage data is sent anywhere.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or manually removing the application data folder. Uninstalling the app removes all stored preferences and credentials.

Children's Privacy

The Colosseum is intended for use by administrators managing game tournaments. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
