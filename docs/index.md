# The Colosseum

A desktop app for running custom video game tournaments end-to-end.

## What it does

The Colosseum manages tournament workflows in one place:

- **Setup** — Configure tournament name, game, date/time, signup deadline, draft style, and player count limits
- **Draft Board** — Sleeper-style team drafting with a pick-wheel randomizer and per-pick timer
- **Bracket Management** — Auto-populate Challonge brackets with team names, dates, times, and stream links
- **Tournament Control** — Start your tournament and trigger updates across all integrated services

## Integrations

- **Google Forms** — Auto-updates your signup form template with tournament details
- **Challonge** — Pushes finalized brackets to your community via REST API; credentials stored securely
